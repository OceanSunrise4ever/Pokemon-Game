**Pocket Quest: 1,000 Wonders**

Pocket Quest is an arcade-like Pokémon adventure built entirely in Python using Pygame.

You can explore the tall grass, battle wild creatures, challenge rare giant Titans, and build an ultimate collection spanning up to 1,000 unique generated species without needing external asset or data downloads. 


## Features

    • 1,000 Species Roster: Combines legendary fan favorites with generated species prefixes and suffixes to provide a massive pool of catchable monsters. 
    
    • Evolution Mechanics: Level up your partner to trigger evolutions at custom level milestones (e.g., Bulbasaur evolving into Ivysaur, then Venusaur). 
    
    • Titan Boss Battles: Every 5 captures triggers a Boss encounter against giant variants like Titan Mewtwo or Titan Charizard. 
        
    • Battle System: Pick your strategy from an 8 move library featuring damage variants, high-risk mystic strikes, and utility heals. 


## How to Play & Controls

**Title Screen**

• Keys 1 to 6: Select your starting partner from the roster (Bulbasaur, Charmander, Squirtle, Pikachu, Eevee, or Ralts) and launch the journey. 

**Exploration Mode**

• WASD or Arrow Keys: Navigate your hero around the camp map.
• Tall Grass: Walk into the central fields to roll random encounters and trigger wild battles.
• Key R: Rest at the base camp to instantly restore your partner's health.
• SPACEBAR: Clear victory banners and resume hunting. 

**Battle Arena**

• Keys 1 to 8: Activate your moves during the player turn.
• SPACEBAR: Pass the turn to the opponent to resolve their counter-attack sequence.
